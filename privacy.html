Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Samaa teaches the oud by ear. This page explains what the app does with your information.

The microphone

Samaa listens to your oud to tell which note you played. That sound is analysed on your own device, while you are in an exercise or the tuner. It is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The optional test recording on the website saves a file to your device only, and you decide whether to share it.

Without an account

Your progress is stored on your device only.

With an account

We store your email address, your password in hashed form, your player name, your country if you choose one, your progress, and a history of the XP you earned. This is kept with our database provider, Supabase.

What other players see

The world leaderboard shows your player name, country if you chose one, Oudi's outfit, your XP and your streak. It never shows your email.

What we do not do

Samaa has no advertising and no third-party tracking, and we do not sell your information.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Profile, then Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your login, progress and leaderboard place. Without an account, use Reset progress in your profile, or uninstall the app.

Children

Samaa accounts are meant for people aged 13 and over.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
